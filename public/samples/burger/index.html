Our story

One griddle. One obsession.

JAMRA means “ember” in Arabic. We started in 2019 with a single steel griddle, a borrowed food truck and a stubborn idea: the best burger is the simplest one, done perfectly.

We still grind our beef every morning, bake our own brioche and smash every patty by hand. No freezers and no heat lamps. Burgers go out the moment they're stacked.
